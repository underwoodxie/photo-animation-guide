# Choosing the Right Photo for AI Animation: A Checklist

The photo you start with matters more than any setting. Run through this list before you upload.

| Check | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Face is clearly visible and in focus | The model needs detail to keep the face consistent |
| Subject faces the camera, or close to it | Side profiles and hidden faces distort more easily |
| One or two people, not a crowd | Every extra face is another chance for artifacts |
| Even lighting, no harsh shadows | Shadows can "move" in strange ways |
| Simple background | Busy backgrounds tend to warp |
| Hands not covering the face | Hands are still the hardest thing for AI to animate |
| Resolution at least 1000px on the short side | Low-res inputs turn into mush |

## Photos that usually work well

- Studio portraits and passport-style shots
- Wedding and graduation portraits
- Well-lit selfies
- Pet photos with the animal looking at the camera

## Photos that usually struggle

- Group shots taken from far away
- Motion-blurred action photos
- Heavily filtered or stylized images
- Tiny thumbnails saved from social media
